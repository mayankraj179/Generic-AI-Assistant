# Travel and Expense Policy

Sample content for the kb_demo_assistant object_storage knowledge source
(uploaded to the kb-demo bucket under policies/).

## Booking travel

Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class; longer flights may be
booked in premium economy with manager approval. All travel must be booked
through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days in advance.

## Daily allowance

The domestic per diem is INR 3,000 per day. The international per diem is
USD 75 per day. Hotel stays are capped at INR 8,000 per night in metro
cities and INR 5,000 per night elsewhere.

## Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from the trip,
with original receipts for every item above INR 500. Claims submitted after
30 days need written approval from the department head.
