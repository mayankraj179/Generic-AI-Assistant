# IT Support Handbook

Sample content for the kb_demo_assistant filesystem knowledge source.

## Passwords

Company account passwords must be at least 14 characters long and expire
every 90 days. The last 12 passwords cannot be reused. Reset a forgotten
password yourself at the self-service portal; the IT helpdesk can reset it
only after verifying your identity over a video call.

## VPN access

Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN client with
multi-factor authentication. VPN sessions disconnect automatically after
10 hours, and idle sessions after 30 minutes.

## Laptops

Laptops are refreshed every 4 years. A lost or stolen laptop must be
reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so the device can be remotely
locked and wiped.
