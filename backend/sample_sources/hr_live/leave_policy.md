# Leave Policy

Sample policy for the hr_live_assistant demo. It describes entitlements and
rules; an employee's own current balance comes from the HR system.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues 21 days of annual leave per calendar year,
at 1.75 days per completed month of service. Annual leave must be applied for
at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the
reporting manager.

At the end of the calendar year, up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be
carried forward into the next year. Any unused annual leave above 10 days
lapses on 31 December and is not paid out. Carried-forward days must be used
by 31 March of the following year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not
carry forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than
2 consecutive days.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per calendar year, for personal
matters that need short notice. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual
leave in the same request and does not carry forward.

## Negative balances

Leave cannot be taken in advance of accrual. A request that would take an
annual, sick or casual leave balance below zero is treated as unpaid leave
and needs HR approval.
